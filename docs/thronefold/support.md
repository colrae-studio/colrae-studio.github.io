# Support

## How to play

Each day, Thronefold gives everyone the same grid. Solve that grid to finish the day. When you want more, follow the path of levels and collect stars.

## Progress

Progress is stored on this device only. Deleting Thronefold deletes it. There is no account to restore it from.

## Ad choices

Thronefold is free with ads. To change your choices, open Settings in the app and look for Privacy choices, or a similarly named option, if it is there. You can also change tracking permission for Thronefold in the iOS Settings app.
