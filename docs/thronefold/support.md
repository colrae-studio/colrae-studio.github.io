# Support

## How to play

Each day, Thronefold gives everyone the same grid. Solve that grid to finish the day. When you want more, follow the path of levels and collect stars.

## Progress

Progress is stored on this device only. Deleting Thronefold deletes it. There is no account to restore it from.

## Ad choices

Thronefold is free with ads. Open Settings in the app and choose Privacy options. That entry is shown where required. You can also change tracking permission for Thronefold in the iOS Settings app.
